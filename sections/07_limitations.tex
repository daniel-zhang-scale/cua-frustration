\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Wall-clock time depends on infrastructure.} Agent time includes model latency, which varies with provider load, region and hardware. We fix the infrastructure across runs and also report action-based budgets, which do not depend on latency. \todo{Report the variance of $T_r$ across repeated runs.}

\paragraph{Tolerance is subjective.} Tolerance varies across people, tasks and contexts: a user watching the screen tolerates less than one who delegates a task and returns later. Our labels describe the attended setting with \tbd{n} annotators per task.

\paragraph{Scale.} 75 tasks support the comparisons we make but not fine-grained claims about individual categories. \todo{Report confidence intervals per category.}

\section*{Ethics Statement}
Annotators were \tbd{contracted and paid at or above local minimum wage}; no personal data about them is recorded or released. Tasks run in isolated virtual machines and locally hosted applications, and no task acts on live third-party services or real user accounts.
